# readable-interpreter

![Status: Incomplete](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Incomplete-orange?style=for-the-badge)

This is just a simple calculator that processes mathematical expressions giving you the end result with error handling. Yes the idea is simple but it allows me to focus more on walking you through the actual working principle of interpreters (compilers on a broader sense).

## So why "READABLE" ?

When i say "readable" you can understand the interpreter's flow just by reading its source code. I used actual words that gives you an idea on why the variable, function, struct, etc is needed and its program flow.
